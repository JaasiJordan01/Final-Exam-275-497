About Bethany of Victory

A Pool of Healing (John 5:2-9) — We are a prophetic church raised to bring wholeness, deliverance, and the light of Christ to defeat darkness. Founded on unceasing prayer, we welcome all angels of light.

Healing Mandate

Jesus heals every sickness — spiritual, physical, emotional. We believe in miracles today.

Fight Darkness

We stand against powers of darkness through worship, prayer, and the blood of Jesus.

Unceasing Prayer

1 Thessalonians 5:17. A 24/7 altar of intercession, praying for Uganda and the world.

Angels of Light

Empowered believers bringing hope, not fear. Walking in authority and love.

What We Believe

📖 The Bible is God's infallible Word. We believe in the Trinity: Father, Son (Jesus Christ) and Holy Spirit. Salvation through faith in Jesus’ sacrifice. We practice water baptism and Holy Communion. We believe in the gifts of the Holy Spirit: healing, prophecy, deliverance. We welcome all who are sick, weary, or oppressed — you shall be made whole. As God said, "Come with all your sicknesses, spiritual or physical, you will get healed."
